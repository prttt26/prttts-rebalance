Energy & Deck Cycling

以能量经济和牌组运转为重点的个人平衡方案。

调整战士、猎人、机器人、亡灵契约师与储君的部分卡牌和卡池，涉及费用、能量获取、抽牌、弃牌、回收和跨回合资源安排；同时引入部分《杀戮尖塔1》经典卡牌及新设计卡牌。

目标是让资源投入与收益更协调，为构筑提供更多运转方式。塔1卡牌回归是其中一部分，亡灵契约师与储君的调整也是本 Mod 的重要内容。

需要订阅并启用 BaseLib。提供简体中文和英文卡牌文本。
本 Mod 会改变游戏玩法。与修改相同卡牌、卡池或遗物的其他 Mod 同时使用时，可能产生冲突。
首个测试版本：v0.1.0。欢迎反馈卡牌效果、描述和兼容性问题。

An opinionated rebalance focused on energy economy and deck cycling.

Adjusts selected cards and card pools for the Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Necrobinder, and Regent: costs, energy generation, draw, discard, retrieval, and resources carried between turns. Includes selected returning Slay the Spire 1 cards and new card designs.

The goal is to make resource costs and rewards more coherent and provide more ways to keep a deck moving. Returning cards are one part of the mod; Necrobinder and Regent adjustments are also a substantial part of its scope.

Requires BaseLib. Includes Simplified Chinese and English card text.
This mod affects gameplay and may conflict with other mods that change the same cards, pools, or relics.
Initial testing release: v0.1.0. Feedback on card behavior, text, and compatibility is welcome.
