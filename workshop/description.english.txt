Energy & Deck Cycling

An opinionated rebalance focused on energy economy and deck cycling.

Adjusts selected cards and card pools: costs, energy generation, draw, discard, retrieval, and resources carried between turns. Includes selected returning Slay the Spire 1 cards and new designs, aiming for more coherent resource costs and rewards and more ways to keep a deck moving.

Requires BaseLib. Includes Simplified Chinese and English card text.
Designed around the main (public) branch. The public-beta branch may differ from the intended balance: Expertise and Fuel use base-game behavior, so beta changes to these cards can affect the intended gameplay. Workshop downloads are not restricted by branch.
This mod affects gameplay and may conflict with other mods that change the same cards, pools, or relics.
Current version: v0.1.0. Feedback on card behavior, text, and compatibility is welcome.
